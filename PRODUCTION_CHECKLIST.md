# Production launch checklist

- [ ] Animal-law counsel and welfare adviser approve US-first scope and supported species.
- [ ] Species x origin x destination eligibility engine; default-deny uncertainty and prohibited species.
- [ ] Seller identity/business verification, permits, captive-bred/source evidence and veterinary records.
- [ ] Buyer age/eligibility, listing moderation, reporting, appeals and incident response.
- [ ] Persistent database, authenticated roles, MFA for sellers/admins, audit logs and backups.
- [ ] Server-authoritative bidding, auction clock, concurrency handling, anti-sniping and cancellation rules.
- [ ] Live video and moderation; secure messaging and privacy controls.
- [ ] Written payment-provider approval for this exact animal marketplace, plus tax, refunds, chargebacks and payout procedures.
- [ ] Legal transport/handoff controls, welfare standards, terms, privacy, insurance and support.
- [ ] Security, accessibility, mobile, load and recovery testing; pilot with vetted sellers only.
